# Worked example: thesis acknowledgement

## Before → After (selected paragraphs)

**Before:** I owe this work to Dr. R. Praveen, Registrar, CMR University, Banaswadi, Bangalore who was instrumental in mentoring me and helping me to pursue this research study, without him, this dream work of mine would not have been possible. I bow to his unstinted, unconditional concern and humility in facilitating the process of research, with lot of composure and patience.

**After:** I owe this work to Dr. R. Praveen, Registrar, CMR University, Banaswadi, Bangalore, who mentored me and encouraged me to pursue this research. Without him, this dream work of mine would not have been possible. I bow to his unstinted concern and his humility. He guided the research process with composure and patience.

*Fixed:* "instrumental", comma splice, "with lot of", "facilitating". *Kept:* "dream work of mine", "I bow to", full designation.

---

**Before:** I would like to express my sincere thanks and gratitude to Prof. Singaravelu and Prof. J. K. Reddy … helping me internalizing the systematic research process. I owe my sincere thanks and gratitude to both of them for being instrumental in completing the work systematically.

**After:** I am deeply grateful to Prof. Singaravelu and Prof. J. K. Reddy of the Department of Education, Bharathiar University, who guided me through the Doctoral Committee Meetings. They helped me internalise the discipline of systematic research, and this work was completed in an orderly way because of them.

*Fixed:* stacked and repeated thanks, "internalizing", "instrumental".

---

**Before:** I have no words to thank Late Prof. K. B. Akhilesh … He was instrumental in helping me understand the application of the Structural Equation Modelling in Educational Research.

**After:** I have no words to thank the late Prof. K. B. Akhilesh, Dean, IISc, Bangalore. … It was he who taught me how Structural Equation Modelling can be applied in educational research.

*Kept:* "I have no words to thank". *Fixed:* "Late" → "the late", "instrumental".

---

**Before:** With deep sense of gratitude and due respect, I bestow my earnest and sincere thanks to Prof. Sudhakar Venu … for his perseverant support…

**After:** With a deep sense of gratitude and respect, I thank Prof. Sudhakar Venu (EFLU, Hyderabad), who patiently guided me through editing and finalising the thesis.

*Fixed:* "bestow" (wrong direction of respect), "perseverant", stacked adjectives.

---

**Before:** I would love to express my lovely thanks to my mentor and husband … Last, but not the least, the study would not have been possible without the blessings of my mother Sukanya…

**After:** My loving thanks go to my mentor and husband, Dr. Niranjanaradhya V. P., and to our daughters … Finally, this study would not have been possible without the blessings of my mother, Sukanya…

*Fixed:* "lovely thanks", "Last, but not the least". *Kept:* "blessings", the family tenderness.

## Please check (flag, don't change)

- Same name in two roles (Prof. Niranjanaradhya V. P. listed among professors and as husband).
- Official institution names and spellings (University of Mysore; Himachal Pradesh University; Uttarakhand; Ramanagara).
- Any rephrasing of a feeling ("inconsistency in caring") — confirm it still feels true.
