# Worked example: research paper (Register 1)

Source: "Implementation of RTE by Gram Panchayat: Challenges and Action Agenda".

## 1. Field finding: vary "It was found that", keep the voices

**Before:** It was found that the majority members including the officials of the GP such as Panchayat Development Officer were unaware of the roles and responsibilities of local authority under the RtE Act. As the preconception is that, it is the responsibility of the education department… More probing during interview only led to their understanding of Rte act as 25% admission to the private institutions.

**After:** Most GP members, including the Panchayat Development Officers, did not know the roles the RTE Act assigns to the local authority. They saw education as the responsibility of the Education Department and the School Management Committees. Even on further probing, their understanding of the Act went no further than the 25% reservation in private schools.

*Fixed:* "It was found that", sentence fragment, RtE/Rte inconsistency. *Kept:* the finding, the 25% detail, the probing.

## 2. Irony: keep the observation, clean the sentence

**Before:** In Karnataka, SDMC's formation is linked with the PRI and is an extended arm of the Gram Panchayat in management of Primary Schools and it is ironical to know that they know nothing of RTE Act.

**After:** In Karnataka, the SDMC is linked to the PRI and acts as an extended arm of the Gram Panchayat in managing primary schools. Ironically, its members know nothing of the RTE Act.

## 3. Puffery: use the plain fact she already gives

**Before:** This judgment ushered in a new era in the field of school education by declaring the right to education as a fundamental right of every child up to the age of 14 years.

**After:** This judgment declared education a fundamental right of every child up to the age of 14.

## 4. "Lack of" chain: group it

**Before:** The core issues are lack of awareness, lack of systemic information flow, … lack of human resources, lack of education, … lack of resources and devolution of powers and finances…

**After:** The core issues are a lack of awareness and of systematic information flow; a gap between legislation and implementation procedures; little participation of GP functionaries in education, who are overloaded with other work; low levels of education; the challenges of decentralising education management; and a lack of human and financial resources, with no devolution of powers and finances to implement RTE at the grassroots.

## 5. Verbless "to be" instruction

**Before:** Orientation to representatives from School, SDMC, GP, TP and ZP to be provided towards carrying out Makkala Grama Sabhe (Children's meeting at GP).

**After:** Representatives of the school, SDMC, GP, TP and ZP should be oriented to conduct the Makkala Grama Sabhe (children's assembly at the Gram Panchayat).

## 6. Homely analogy: keep it

**Before:** Mere assigning the roles and responsibilities without any fund allocation and empowerment is like assigning the job of chewing without the provision of teeth.

**After:** Assigning roles and responsibilities without funds or powers is like assigning the job of chewing without providing teeth.

*Kept:* the analogy, which is concrete and argues the point. Only the grammar is tightened.
