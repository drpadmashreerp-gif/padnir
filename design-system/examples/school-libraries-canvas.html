Space and infrastructure

<15%

of schools have computers or tablets

Many schools report a library, but most have no room of their own. Books sit in classrooms, cupboards, the headmaster's room or temporary spaces. Racks, reading tables and seating are short, and features for children with disabilities are largely absent.

What can change now

Quick steps, then lasting investment

  • Train library in-charges.
  • Make books accessible to children.
  • Give library management a formal place in the school.
