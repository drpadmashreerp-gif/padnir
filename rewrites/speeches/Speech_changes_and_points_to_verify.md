# Chief Secretary's speech, 04-11-2026: changes and points to verify

## What was removed (AI patterns and stagecraft)

- **The hand-raising exercise and stage directions** ("[Pause. Look around the hall. Smile.]"). These are motivational-speaker devices, out of place for a Chief Secretary at a meeting chaired by the Hon'ble Chief Minister.
- **Slogan triplets and staged reveals:** "a clear promise, a clear plan, clean systems"; "One School. One Hour. One Measure."; "here is the hopeful part"; "Do you see?"; "That is what partnership looks like."; "Belief, friends, is a resource. And it costs nothing."
- **The imagined-girl story and the "someone believed in you" call-back** at the close.
- **Dramatic repetition** ("Sixty-five per cent." on its own line) and the "not X but Y" constructions, along with every em dash.

## Tone adopted

The tone is measured, factual and courteous. It thanks the partners, states the problem plainly, sets out what the Government has already done, and makes a specific request. The Kannada lines are kept, along with the three requests (adopt a school, give an hour a month, agree on a measure), now stated plainly rather than as a slogan.
