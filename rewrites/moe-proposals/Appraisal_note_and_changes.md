## Additions for DSERT to confirm

1. **Summary paragraph** at the start of each proposal (drawn only from the proposal's own content).
2. **"Relationship with the companion proposal"** section in both, and a matching sentence in each Certificate. Both studies evaluate in-service training from 2021 to 2026 with classroom observation, so an appraiser will ask about duplication. The section commits DSERT to sharing training records, keeping school samples separate and holding a joint Steering Committee review.
3. **Power calculation** at inception, using intra-class correlations from the pilot (both proposals).
4. **Sampling rules** in the training-effectiveness proposal: the observed teacher teaches Grade 3, 5 or 8, and replacement schools are drawn from the same stratum.
5. **Milestone-linked release of funds** (both proposals).

## Points to verify before submission

- **Number of DIETs.** The training-effectiveness proposal covers 30 DIET districts; the integrated evaluation covers 34 DIETs and 34 educational districts. Please confirm the correct figure for each study. If the 30-district coverage is deliberate, give the reason.
- **Combined cost.** The two proposals together cost ₹2,24,45,325. An appraiser may ask whether shared fieldwork could reduce cost.
- **Period evaluated.** The records in Annexure I begin in 2022-23. The rewrite now says that 2021-22 records will be compiled in Component 1.
- **Unit costs** are described as indicative and to be finalised against the Samagra Shiksha norms in force at approval.
