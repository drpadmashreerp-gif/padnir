# Investment to Impact proposal: consistency check and changes

## Figures checked and found consistent

- **Budget:** all 33 line items, head totals and the Year 1 and Months 13-18 sub-totals, plus the 5 per cent contingency (₹5,77,445 and ₹1,26,150 before rounding; shown as ₹5,77,500 and ₹1,26,200, as the table states).
- **Totals and shares:** the grand total of ₹1,47,75,600, the 60:40 shares (₹88,65,360 / ₹59,10,240), the share of each head, and the amount in words.
- **Sample counts:** 34 × 18 = 612 schools; 612 × 4 = 2,448 teachers; 612 × 15 = 9,180 students; 34 × 6 = 204 cohort teachers; 34 × 7 = 238 CRPs and BRPs; 68 live sessions and 68 FGDs; 170 + 10 = 180 interviews; 68 + 180 = 248 transcripts; 68 investigators, 68 tablets and 68 data allowances.
- **Tests:** 2,448 sample teachers + (204 cohort teachers × 2) = 2,856 knowledge tests.
- **Power figures:** with one classroom per school, 18 per district and a district intra-class correlation of 0.05, the minimum detectable difference is 0.31 SD for practice. With four teachers per school and correlations of 0.15 (school) and 0.05 (district), it is 0.25 SD for knowledge. These match the 0.30 and 0.25 SD stated.
- **Progress records:** the progress records (Section 2, Annexures I and II) match the corrected figures in the earlier proposal.
- **Timelines:** Steering Committee meetings (6), monitoring visits (2 per district), the pilot (10 schools) and the training of trainers (50 participants) match the budget.
